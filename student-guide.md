# Student Guide: Getting Started

## Step 1: Log in

1. Open the link provided by your instructor in your browser.
2. You will see a login page. Fill in:
   - **UID**: Your email address (e.g., `yourname@example.com`)
   - **Name**: Your full name
   - **UIN**: Your student number (optional)
3. Click **Dev Mode Login**.

Your account is created automatically on first login. Use the **same UID every time** you log in — this is how the system identifies you.

## Step 2: Enroll in the course

After logging in, visit the course link provided by your instructor:

**`http://<host>:3000/pl/course_instance/5`**

You will be enrolled automatically and see the list of assessments.

## Step 3: Take assessments

Click on an assessment to begin. Follow the instructions on each page. Some assessments have multiple sections (zones) that must be completed in order.

## Important

- **Remember your UID.** If you log in with a different UID, you will appear as a different student and lose access to your previous work.
- There is no password. Please only use your own UID.
